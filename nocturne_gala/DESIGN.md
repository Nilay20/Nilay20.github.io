## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an avant-garde cinematic gala experience, merging ceremonial luxury with futuristic bioluminescent illumination. The target audience comprises discerning patrons, curators, cultural visionaries, and VIP guests attending exclusive milestone galas, prestige awards, and immersive premier events. 

The aesthetic pushes celebratory glamour into an experimental, theatrical realm. It rejects stale, traditional ballroom tropes in favor of deep-space midnight architecture flooded with hyper-saturated amber luminescence, sweeping curvilinear glass capsules, and striking typography. The resulting emotional tone is reverent, dramatic, luminous, and undeniably exclusive—evoking the anticipation of a darkened auditorium illuminated by a single warm golden spotlight.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in absolute abyss neutrals and elevated through warm chromatic luminescence:

- **Neutral Foundations:** `#080A10` serves as the primary canvas ground, complemented by `#0B0F19` for elevated surface backdrops. These deep navy-tinted midnight shades absorb ambient light, providing maximum contrast for glowing artifacts.
- **Primary Luminescence (`#FFB347`):** Radiant amber serves as the active pulse of the interface, delivering high-energy focal points, bioluminescent back-glows, and key interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Champagne (`#D4AF37`):** An opulent, refined metallic tone used for structural filigree, fine frosted borders, badges, and decorative accents.
- **Tertiary Flare (`#FF8C00`):** Intense ember orange reserved for energetic notifications, critical interactive states, and edge lighting gradients.

### Functional Roles & Gradients
- **Surface Overlays:** Translucent layers utilizing `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.72)` combined with frosted glass diffusion.
- **Golden Glows:** Subtle radial gradients (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.15) 0%, transparent 70%)`) deployed behind active cards and hero media.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text renders in crisp champagne white (`#FBF8F2`), secondary supporting text in muted pearl (`#A3A8B8`), and disabled items in deep smoked bronze (`#4A4639`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the avant-garde, expressive geometry of Syne with the structural legibility of Manrope.

- **Syne** commands attention across all display and headline levels. Its sculpted, sculptural proportions capture the drama of theatrical programs, gala invitations, and grand title cards.
- **Manrope** provides a balanced, high-legibility foundation for body copy, micro-interactions, lists, and metadata.
- **Kerning and Case:** Upper labels and badges (`label-sm`, `label-lg`) mandate uppercase rendering with increased letter tracking (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to mirror premium editorial plaques. Headlines feature tight tracking to accentuate geometric impact.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an asymmetrical fluid grid with deliberate theatrical pacing. Negative space is deployed intentionally as a luxury commodity, allowing individual glass cards and glowing content pillars room to breathe.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on desktop screens with `gutter-lg` (2.5rem) and maximum container widths capped at `1440px`. Mobile viewports compress to a fluid 4-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem).
- **Margins:** Outer canvas borders utilize adaptive margins (`margin` on mobile up to `margin-lg` on wide screens) to maintain an expansive, border-conscious stage.
- **Rhythm:** The spacing scale centers on a base unit of 8px, utilizing generous padding (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) within translucent cards to preserve an airy, cinematic composition.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on bioluminescent amber back-lighting layered beneath frosted glassmorphism:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Base Canvas:** Solid midnight black (`#080A10`).
  - **Layer 1 (Atmosphere):** Radial particle and amber glow filters (`filter: blur(80px)`) drifting beneath interactive elements.
  - **Layer 2 (Capsules & Cards):** Translucent deep navy fill (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.65)`) with an inner background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`).
  - **Layer 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** High-density smoked glass (`rgba(16, 21, 36, 0.85)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(40px)`.
- **Borders & Rims:** Glass surfaces are defined by a continuous 1px micro-border utilizing a linear gradient running from champagne gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`) down to faint transparency (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Ambient Illumination:** Interactive cards omit harsh cast drop shadows. Instead, elevated items cast a soft, diffused amber flare (`box-shadow: 0 12px 40px -10px rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.18)`), which intensifies upon hover.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is defined by fluid pill silhouettes and sweeping curvature (roundedness tier `3`). 

- **Primary Radius:** Core interface controls, chips, navigation pills, and interactive inputs adhere to full pill shapes (`rounded-full` or `9999px`).
- **Cards & Enclosures:** Large media displays, stage program capsules, and event containers employ exaggerated pill-inspired radiuses (`rounded-xl` at 3rem / 48px). Smaller nested modules utilize 1rem to 2rem rounding to maintain organic continuity.
- **Geometry:** Hard right angles are avoided. The silhouette language suggests polished stones, flowing liquid champagne, and organic optical lenses.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Luminescent Gold):** Solid gradient fill between `#FFB347` and `#FF8C00`, styled as a full pill shape (`rounded-full`). Text set in bold Manrope (`#080A10`). Hover triggers a luminous outer aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.45)`) and slight upward translation.
- **Secondary (Glass Champagne):** Pill-shaped frosted container (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`) with a 1px border of `#D4AF37`. Champagne text (`#F7D070`). Hover shifts background to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless pill with subtle amber text, displaying a localized gold underline flare on focus.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact pill geometries (`padding: 0.375rem 1rem`) utilizing translucent navy glass (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.7)`).
- Bordered by a delicate 1px line of `rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.3)`. Contains an optional bioluminescent glowing micro-dot indicator (4px amber circle with CSS pulsing animation).

### Cards & Program Pods
- Translucent capsule modules built with `3rem` rounded corners, backdrop-blur (`24px`), and gradient stroke rims.
- Backgrounds host ambient amber spotlights that follow cursor coordinates or stay anchored under celebratory imagery.
- Generous internal padding (`space-xl`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Pill-shaped input fields (`height: 52px`, `padding: 0 1.5rem`) rendered in dark smoked glass (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.5)`).
- Resting state features a faint champagne border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`). 
- Active focus state expands into a glowing amber border (`#FFB347`) accompanied by a subtle inner glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.1)`). Placeholder text in muted bronze-gray.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular pill-matched discs. When active, displays a solid midnight core surrounded by an illuminated `#FFB347` ring with soft outer luminescence.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squircle capsules (8px radius) featuring a golden champagne tick mark when active, set over an amber gradient fill.

### List Items & Itineraries
- Divided not by solid lines, but by subtle horizontal hairline gradients fading out at the edges (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2), transparent)`).
- Hover states transition the individual row into a pill-contoured glass highlight.

### Gala Spotlight Elements (Custom)
- **VIP Golden Lanyards:** Floating pill tags tethered to attendee profiles with metallic champagne gradient borders.
- **Particle Canvases:** Embedded background particle arrays that emit slowly drifting gold embers beneath high-tier celebration announcements.
